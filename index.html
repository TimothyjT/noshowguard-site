What it does

  1. A customer books on a booking page we build for your business — your services, your hours, your deposit policy.

  2. They put down a small deposit, an amount you set, through your own Stripe payment link. The money goes to your Stripe account, and card details never touch our system. Until the deposit lands, the slot is held, not confirmed — and the customer is told the deadline up front.

  3. The confirmation email goes out right away, and a reminder email lands two days before the appointment. If the customer cancels or reschedules, the sequence stops or re-times itself. If they opt out, they are never messaged again.

Sample moment

Sample: a customer books a haircut and color for Tuesday at 10:30 AM and puts $20 down. Her confirmation says the slot is held, that the deposit is credited in full when she shows up, and that it is kept only if she no-shows or cancels inside 24 hours. Two days before the appointment, the reminder lands with the same policy in the same plain words.

The deposit, in plain words

The deposit is the part that does the work. A customer with money down rearranges their day. A customer with nothing down loses nothing by vanishing. Here is exactly how it treats your customers:

  • Credited in full at the appointment. It comes off what they owe on the day. It is a hold, not an extra charge.
  • Kept only on a no-show, or a cancel inside the window you set — 24 hours is typical. That window is yours.
  • Outside the window, nothing is kept. The deposit goes back to the customer's original payment method through Stripe, on the timing your policy states.
  • Your customer reads the whole policy before paying anything, and you approve the exact wording first. If you wouldn't say it to a regular's face, we don't ship it.
  • If the deposit never lands, the customer gets one reminder email halfway through the hold; then the hold is released, the slot opens back up, and you are told.

Don't want deposits at all? The gate switches off and the reminders still run, at the same price. But the deposit is the mechanism this product is built around.

About the text reminders — the honest asterisk

Two of the reminders in the sequence are text messages: one a day before, one two hours before. They are not live yet, and this page won't pretend otherwise. Live texting requires carrier (A2P) registration, typically 2–5 business days, and the texts stay disabled — not queued quietly — until that registration is approved and a live delivery test for your business is on record. Email runs from day one, and we will tell you in writing which channels are live. The texts are included in the same $29/mo when they switch on; there is no second charge for them.

Who it's for — and who it's not

It's for you if…

  • You book appointments ahead — salon chairs, treatment rooms, cleaning crews — and an empty slot costs you real money.
  • Your reminders today are a sticky note, a hope, or nothing.
  • You already take payment through Stripe, or you're willing to start for the deposits.

It's not for you if…

  • Your bookings are walk-in only. There is no slot to protect.
  • Your booking can't move to the page we provide. This version runs on that page only — if that doesn't work for your business, we'll tell you before you pay.

In this version No-Show Guard does not sit on top of an existing booking system, and it does not sync with an outside calendar. Feeding it bookings from another system's exports is a later version — we won't sell it to you before it exists.

What it deliberately doesn't do

  • It doesn't promise a number. Nobody can honestly tell you "this cuts no-shows by X%" — we have no such data and won't invent one. What's on offer is the mechanism, at a price you can judge against your first month of bookings.
  • It doesn't touch your money. Deposits go through your own Stripe payment link to your own Stripe account. There is no card field anywhere in this system, and we never hold a customer's card details.
  • It doesn't message strangers. Only people who booked with you ever hear from it, and only about that booking. No marketing sends, no cold lists.
