What's included

  • The deposit gate, wired to your own Stripe payment link — your amount, your policy window, the policy wording shown to the customer before they pay
  • A booking page built for your business — your services, your availability, your deposit policy. This is the one supported workflow in this version
  • A confirmation email the moment a booking exists, and a reminder email two days before every appointment
  • Text reminders a day out and two hours out — included in the same price, switched on only after carrier (A2P) registration is approved and a live delivery test for your business is on record
  • Cancel and reschedule handling: the sequence stops or re-times itself, and opt-outs are honored for good
  • An owner view: upcoming bookings, deposit status on each, reminder status, and a flag on any send that fails
  • Reminder wording written from your business details and approved by you before go-live
  • A live test booking run through your own setup before go-live, with the evidence shown to you
  • A monthly summary: bookings covered, reminders sent, deposits taken, credited, and kept

Guarantee: Live by the date we agree, or your setup fee comes back in full — guaranteed.

The go-live clock starts when payment, your content, and access are all in — whichever comes last starts the clock.
